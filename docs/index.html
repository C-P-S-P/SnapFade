SnapFade

Screenshots that disappear.

Take a screenshot. It goes to your clipboard, not your gallery. Paste it anywhere. Gone in hours. Worth keeping? One tap saves it forever.

Power + Vol Down → clipboard → paste anywhere
Intercept Uses the default screenshot shortcut. No new gesture to learn.
Clipboard Screenshot goes straight to clipboard. Gallery stays clean.
Fade Auto-deletes after 1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours. You pick.
Save Popup appears after every capture. Tap Save to keep it forever.
Share Share directly from the app. No save-then-find-then-share loop.
Tiny 2 MB. Four Kotlin files. No ads, no analytics, no internet.

Setup

Install the APK and open SnapFade.

Enable the accessibility service in Settings. On Android 13+ sideloads: App Info → Allow restricted settings first.

Grant "All files access" so SnapFade can remove screenshots from the gallery.
